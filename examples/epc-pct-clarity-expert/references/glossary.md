# Glossary — EPC / PCT Patent Practice

Key terminology used in this skill, English with Italian equivalents. Terms in **bold** are particularly central to clarity practice.

> Style: definitions are operational, not encyclopaedic. Each term cites the relevant article/rule and where used in this skill.

---

## A

**Added matter** (IT: *materia aggiunta*) — Subject-matter introduced into an application by amendment that was not directly and unambiguously disclosed in the application as filed. Prohibited under **Art. 123(2) EPC**. See [related-provisions.md](related-provisions.md).

**Amendment** (IT: *emendamento / modifica*) — Change to claims, description, or drawings during prosecution. Governed by **Rule 137 EPC** during examination; **Rule 80 EPC** in opposition.

**Article 54(5) EPC** — Second medical use provision (EPC 2000). Allows claims of the form "Substance X for use in the treatment of disease Y".

**Article 69 EPC** — Governs the **extent of protection** (used for infringement). Read with the Protocol on Interpretation. Distinct from Art. 84 which governs examination.

**Article 83 EPC** — **Sufficiency of disclosure**. The application must disclose the invention sufficiently for the skilled person to carry it out.

**Article 84 EPC** — **Claims** must define the invention, be clear, be concise, and be supported. The central provision of this skill.

**Article 123(2) EPC** — **No added matter** by amendment. The "gold standard": amendments must be directly and unambiguously derivable from the application as filed.

**Article 123(3) EPC** — Post-grant, amendments cannot **extend the scope of protection**.

**Auxiliary request** (IT: *richiesta ausiliaria*) — A fallback amended set of claims, filed alongside the main request, that the EPO will consider if the main request fails. Frequent in oral proceedings.

---

## B

**Board of Appeal** (IT: *Commissione di ricorso*) — Quasi-judicial second-instance body at the EPO. Decisions cited as "T XXX/YY" (Technical Board), "J XXX/YY" (Legal Board).

**Boards of Appeal Rules of Procedure (RPBA)** — Procedural rules for the Boards. Most recent: RPBA 2020, in force from 1 January 2020.

---

## C

**Categorisation (claim category)** (IT: *categoria di rivendicazione*) — A claim is one of: product, process, method, use, apparatus. See **Rule 43(2) EPC** for restrictions on multiple independents of the same category.

**CLBA** (Case Law of the Boards of Appeal of the European Patent Office) — The "White Book", the authoritative compendium of Board case law published by the EPO. Current edition: 10th, 2022. Free PDF available from epo.org.

**Clarity** (IT: *chiarezza*) — Art. 84 requirement. The claim must define its scope unambiguously to the skilled person. See [article-84-epc.md](article-84-epc.md).

**Conciseness** (IT: *concisione*) — Art. 84 requirement. Both per-claim and across the claim set.

---

## D

**Defining function** (IT: *funzione di definizione*) — Art. 84 requirement that the claim define the matter for which protection is sought. Aspirations and effects alone do not satisfy this.

**Dependent claim** (IT: *rivendicazione dipendente*) — A claim incorporating all features of another claim by reference, adding further features. Rule 43(3)(4) EPC.

**Description adaptation** (IT: *adattamento della descrizione*) — Practice of amending the description to be consistent with allowed claims. Currently controversial — see [case-law-clarity.md](case-law-clarity.md) and the **T 1989/18 vs T 1473/19** split, pending **G 1/24**.

**Designated states** (IT: *Stati designati*) — Member states in which a European patent application takes effect. Now automatic for all EPC contracting states (since EPC 2000).

**Disclaimer** (IT: *clausola di esclusione / disclaimer*) — Negative limitation excluding subject-matter from a claim. Categories: **disclosed** (G 2/10 gold standard) and **undisclosed** (G 1/03 conditions).

**Doctrine of equivalents** (IT: *dottrina degli equivalenti*) — National-court doctrine extending claim scope beyond literal wording for infringement purposes. Not relevant to Art. 84 examination at EPO.

---

## E

**Enlarged Board of Appeal** (IT: *Alta Commissione di ricorso allargata*) — Highest EPO body. Decides referrals from Boards on points of fundamental importance. Decisions: "G X/YY".

**EPC** — European Patent Convention. Treaty establishing the European Patent Office (EPO). Current text: 17th edition (2020), incorporating EPC 2000 revisions.

**EPO** — European Patent Office, based in Munich with branches in The Hague, Berlin, Vienna. Examines European patent applications under the EPC.

**Essential feature** (IT: *caratteristica essenziale*) — A feature necessary to solve the technical problem the invention addresses. Omission from the independent claim → Art. 84 objection (F-IV-4.5).

**European patent attorney (EPA)** (IT: *Mandatario europeo*) — Qualified representative before the EPO. Listed on the EPO professional representatives' list per Art. 134 EPC.

**Examining Division** (IT: *Divisione di esame*) — EPO first-instance body for substantive examination. Typically three-member.

---

## F

**Functional feature** (IT: *caratteristica funzionale*) — A claim feature defined by what it does rather than what it is ("means for", "configured to"). See [clarity-objections-catalog.md § 4](clarity-objections-catalog.md#4-functional-features).

---

## G

**G 1/03 + G 2/03** — Enlarged Board decisions on **undisclosed disclaimers** (2004). Conditions: restore novelty over Art. 54(3), accidental anticipation, or remove Art. 53 subject-matter.

**G 2/10** — Enlarged Board decision on **disclosed disclaimers** (2011). Gold standard applies.

**G 3/14** — Enlarged Board decision (2015) — **clarity in opposition**. Claims as granted not examinable for Art. 84; amended claims examinable only for the amendment-introduced clarity issue.

**G 1/16** — Reaffirms two-standard approach for disclaimers (2017).

**G 1/24** (pending) — Description adaptation referral from T 56/21.

**G 2/21** — Plausibility / post-published evidence (2023).

**Gold standard** — The Art. 123(2) test: amendments must be directly and unambiguously derivable from the application as filed. From G 2/10.

**Guidelines for Examination** (IT: *Direttive per l'Esame*) — Internal EPO instructions to examiners. Updated annually. Persuasive but not binding on Boards. Part F Chapter IV covers Claims.

---

## I

**IPEA** (International Preliminary Examining Authority) — PCT authority issuing Chapter II preliminary examination reports.

**ISA** (International Searching Authority) — PCT authority issuing the international search report and written opinion at Chapter I.

**Independent claim** (IT: *rivendicazione indipendente*) — A claim that does not refer back to another claim for its features.

**Intended use** (IT: *uso previsto*) — Use language in an apparatus claim ("apparatus for"). Non-limiting per F-IV-4.13 if the apparatus could perform the use.

---

## M

**Markush group** (IT: *gruppo Markush*) — Claim format defining alternatives sharing a common structural element, typical in chemistry. Rule 6.5 PCT; F-IV-3.6 EPO Guidelines.

**Means-plus-function** — US-style functional claiming. Accepted at EPO under restrictive conditions (F-IV-4.5).

---

## N

**Novelty** (IT: *novità*) — Art. 54 EPC. Distinct from clarity but a claim must be clear for novelty to be assessable.

---

## O

**OJ EPO** — Official Journal of the EPO. Publishes Enlarged Board decisions, Guidelines updates, fee schedules.

**Opposition** (IT: *opposizione*) — Post-grant procedure under Art. 99 EPC. Available for 9 months from grant. Grounds: Art. 100(a)/(b)/(c). Clarity (Art. 84) NOT a ground per G 3/14.

---

## P

**Parameter** (IT: *parametro*) — A measurable characteristic used in a claim (e.g. melting point, particle size, viscosity). Unusual parameters under F-IV-4.11.

**PCT** — Patent Cooperation Treaty (1970). Centralised international filing system. Administered by WIPO.

**Preamble / characterising portion** — Two parts of a Rule 43(1) EPC two-part claim. Preamble: prior-art features. Characterising portion: new features.

**Priority** (IT: *priorità*) — Art. 87 EPC. Right to claim earlier filing date based on first-filing in a Paris Convention country.

**Product-by-process** (IT: *prodotto-per-procedimento*) — Claim defining a product by the process used to make it. Admissible only when product is not otherwise definable (F-IV-4.12).

**Protocol on Interpretation of Article 69** — Companion text to EPC governing claim interpretation for infringement.

---

## R

**Reach-through claim** (IT: *rivendicazione reach-through*) — Claim defining compounds by their interaction with an unrelated target. Typically refused under Art. 83 + Art. 84.

**Reference signs** (IT: *segni di riferimento*) — Drawing numbers in parentheses in claims. Required where drawings exist (Rule 43(7) EPC). **Not limiting** of scope per same Rule.

**Result to be achieved** (IT: *risultato da raggiungere*) — Claim defined by effect alone, without features producing it. Generally objectionable under F-IV-4.10.

**Rule 43 EPC** — Form and content of claims. See [rule-43-epc.md](rule-43-epc.md).

**Rule 6 PCT** — Form and content of claims (international). See [rule-43-epc.md](rule-43-epc.md).

**Rule 63 EPC** — Incomplete search where claims are too unclear. See [rule-43-epc.md § Rule 63](rule-43-epc.md#rule-63-epc--incomplete-search).

**Rule 137 EPC** — Amendment procedure during examination.

---

## S

**Selection invention** (IT: *invenzione di selezione*) — A claim selecting a sub-range / sub-group from a broader disclosure. Must be novel and supported (F-IV-6.3).

**Skilled person** (IT: *persona esperta del ramo*) — The notional addressee of the patent. Test for clarity (Art. 84), sufficiency (Art. 83), inventive step (Art. 56).

**Sufficiency** (IT: *sufficienza di descrizione*) — Art. 83 EPC. The skilled person must be able to carry out the invention without undue burden.

**Support** (IT: *supporto*) — Art. 84 second arm. The claim's full breadth must be commensurate with the description.

**Swiss-form claim** — Pre-EPC 2000 form for second medical use ("Use of compound X for the manufacture of a medicament for treating disease Y"). Replaced by Art. 54(5) EPC form post-EPC 2000.

---

## T

**T XXX/YY** — Technical Board of Appeal decision citation format.

**Trademark in claim** (IT: *marchio nella rivendicazione*) — Discouraged (F-IV-4.8). Acceptable only with constant well-defined composition.

**Two-part form** — Rule 43(1) EPC claim structure with preamble + "characterised in that" + characterising portion.

---

## U

**UPC** — Unified Patent Court. Started operations 1 June 2023. Handles infringement and revocation of European patents with unitary effect and of classical EP patents (with opt-out option). Not relevant to EPO examination but very relevant to ultimate enforcement.

**Undue burden** — Standard for Art. 83 sufficiency. Skilled person should not need to perform inventive effort or extensive experimentation to carry out the invention.

**Unitary patent** (IT: *brevetto unitario*) — European patent with unitary effect (EPUE), in force in participating EU member states since 1 June 2023. Single renewal fee, single litigation forum (UPC).

**Unity of invention** (IT: *unità d'invenzione*) — Art. 82 EPC. A single inventive concept per application.

---

## W

**WIPO** — World Intellectual Property Organization. Administers the PCT.
